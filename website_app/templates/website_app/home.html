I'm a software developer working mostly in Python. Day to day I build LLM systems: FastAPI backends, agent pipelines on LangGraph, MCP servers, and knowledge graphs assembled from embeddings and Neo4j.

The rest of the time it's hardware — embedded builds, vintage gadgets, a home that runs itself, and a workspace I tune more than is reasonable. Some of those projects ended up on Hackaday and XDA Developers. Open source by default, Unix by preference.

I came to software from the sea. Before this I spent years as a senior electrical officer on merchant ships.
